What is Education World Travel?

I love to travel to educate my children. I get educated right along with them! It’s so much more interesting to see the world when you know what you are looking at. When you’ve read a story to your children or they’ve seen something in a movie, and they see that in real life, it’s a magical experience and it sets them up for life-long learning.

Do you want this experience for your children? Are they on holidays from school or are you homeschooling? Do you worldschool? Welcome! Join me as we explore places together and get the most out of them for your family.

About Me

I am an Educational Speech Pathologist, and also a mum to four beautiful boys – three of whom are all grown up now! We live in Australia, and love traveling here and around the world. I have homeschooled my children for many years and always look for ways to educate them (and myself) whenever we travel. If this is your passion too…welcome aboard!
